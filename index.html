Sanity Gauge — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sanity Gauge mobile application (package: com.pokaapps.sanitygauge), developed by Dan Poka. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about how data is handled.

Summary: Sanity Gauge does not require user accounts, does not collect personal identifiers, does not track your physical location, and does not sell or share personal information with third-party advertisers. All data transmitted between the App and our backend services is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

1. Information We Process and How It Is Used

Sanity Gauge is an entertainment application designed to evaluate hypothetical ideas and provide comedic feedback. To deliver this functionality, the App processes the following data:

  • Audio Data & Speech Input (Microphone): When you tap the microphone button, the App requests permission to record audio (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO). Speech-to-text conversion is handled strictly on-device using the native Android Speech Recognizer. Raw audio recordings are never collected, stored, or uploaded to our servers or third parties.
  • User Input Text (Ideas for Evaluation): The scenario text you type or transcribe is transmitted securely via HTTPS to our serverless backend proxy (Supabase Edge Functions) to request comedic evaluation and risk scoring from Google Gemini AI models. Submissions are processed ephemerally to produce the score and roast, and are not tied to user accounts.
  • Anonymous App Activity & Telemetry: To improve app stability, optimize performance, and understand user interest in potential features, the App logs anonymous app interactions (e.g., feature clicks, dialog views, and general risk score range) to our secure database (Supabase). This telemetry is completely anonymous and does not contain personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or persistent hardware identifiers.

2. Third-Party Services and Infrastructure

The App relies on trusted third-party cloud infrastructure to operate:

  • Google Gemini API: Used to generate the comedy evaluation, score, and roast. Queries are transmitted securely in accordance with Google Cloud API terms. Under standard Google Cloud API policies, user prompts submitted through the API are not used to train Google models.
  • Supabase: Acts as our secure serverless backend proxy and telemetry host. All traffic is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

3. Data Security and Encryption

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard data. All communication between the App, Supabase Edge Functions, and the Google Gemini API is encrypted in transit using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). We do not store sensitive personal information or financial data.

4. Permissions Used

  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO: Optional. Used exclusively for on-demand speech-to-text input when you tap the microphone button. You can use the app fully by typing if you prefer not to grant microphone access.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate with the backend proxy to generate AI evaluations and log anonymous telemetry.

5. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

We do not maintain user profiles, account databases, or persistent records of personal data. Anonymous interaction telemetry is retained only for analytical purposes. If you have questions regarding data handling or wish to request the deletion of any data associated with your inquiries, please contact us at pokaapps.dev@gmail.com. We will review and process your request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Sanity Gauge is designed strictly for entertainment purposes and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
